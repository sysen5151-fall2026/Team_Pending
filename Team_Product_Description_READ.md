# Biodiversity Risk Map
Team members: Chengyue Ji, Ling Song, and Zidong Zhang, Ruhong Sheng, Yunqi Cui
## Overview
Biodiversity Risk Map is a decision-support website that helps local
planners, conservation organizations, and environmental researchers
identify areas facing ecological risk. The product combines information on
species observations, habitat conditions, land-cover change, human
disturbance, and protected-area boundaries to calculate a transparent risk
score for each geographic area. Users can explore an interactive map,
filter locations by risk level or habitat type, examine the factors
contributing to each score, and export priority areas for further
investigation. The initial prototype will focus on Tompkins County, NY, and
is intended as a screening tool to support field surveys and conservation
planning.
## Problem
The Biodiversity Risk Map serves local governments, conservation
organizations, land managers, and researchers. These users need an
efficient way to identify locations where valuable ecosystems or species
may be threatened by habitat loss, development, human disturbance, or
insufficient protection.
## Key Features
A typical use case involves a county planner reviewing a proposed
development project in Tompkins County. The planner uses the interactive
map to locate the proposed site, examine its biodiversity risk score, and
identify the factors contributing to that score. If the location has high
species importance, significant habitat pressure, and limited protection,
the planner can export a summary report and recommend a field survey or
further environmental review. This directs limited conservation resources
to the areas that need the most attention.
